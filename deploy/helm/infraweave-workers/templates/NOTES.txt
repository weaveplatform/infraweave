The worker controller of release {{ .Release.Name }} reconciles the WorkerPools of:
{{- range .Values.watchNamespaces }}
  - {{ . }}
{{- end }}

Each WorkerPool reports its binding, registered slots and executor in its
status:

  kubectl get workerpools.workers.infraweave.io -n <namespace> -o wide

Uninstalling deletes the release's WorkerPools first and waits, up to
{{ .Values.uninstall.timeoutSeconds }} seconds, while the controller lets their runs end and
deregisters their workers. The CRDs stay; remove them only once no
WorkerPool or Worker remains in the cluster.
